description: "Steps & Beasts is a gamified step tracker built entirely with Flutter. Every day, users receive a mysterious egg — if they reach their step goal, it hatches into a unique, adorable creature. With over 170 collectable Beasts, evolving rewards, and streak-based rarity, the app turns daily movement into a magical adventure.

What sets Steps & Beasts apart is its blend of playful design, pixel-perfect animations, and meaningful motivation. It uses only on-device storage, works without GPS, and features HomeWidgets and dynamic eggs that visually change state as steps are collected — all powered by native integrations in Flutter.

Built solo, designed with love, and crafted to bring joy to walking – Steps & Beasts shows what’s possible when Flutter meets imagination."
